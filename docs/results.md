# Results

All numbers are generated by the scripts named in the README and stored in `results/`. Figures are in `figures/`
(each has a `.txt` caption). Lengths are in units of the cylinder radius ($a=1$) and frequencies in $c/a$.

## Tier 2 (primary investigation): cylinder array, $\varepsilon_r=4$, normal incidence

### Survey of the reflectance (`experiments/tier2/pitch_study.py`)

Twenty runs (TM and TE, ten pitches 2.2–5.0, resolution 40, 551 frequencies in 0.05–0.60). The reflectance maps
(`figures/tier2_pitch_maps.png`) show, for TE, broad bands with $R=1.000$ that follow the pitch smoothly, and, for TM,
narrow lines at short pitch and a broadening band at long pitch. Lossless energy conservation $|1-R-T|<10^{-2}$ holds for
all peaks except those flagged in the table below.

**Quality flags.** TM runs at $\Lambda=2.2$–2.8 did not decay to the $10^{-7}$ threshold within 1500 time units (high-Q
modes) and the 551-point grid under-samples their lines (frequency step 10⁻³ vs. widths of 3×10⁻⁴ or smaller).
The coarse survey is therefore **not** used for the TM short-pitch conclusions; see the next two sections.

### Lowest strong reflection resonance (`resonance_vs_pitch.py`, `highq_check.py`)

Definition: the lowest-frequency reflection peak with $R_\text{peak}>0.3$ found in the survey. For each pitch the peak
is re-simulated in a 321-point window ($\pm25\%$) and fitted with the coupled-mode form. For TM at $\Lambda\le2.8$ the
narrow mode is taken from a 4000-time-unit Harminv ringdown and confirmed in a 161-point reflectance window of
width ±0.5 %.

Reading the table:

* The lowest resonance of both polarisations falls as the pitch grows (the lattice resonance is pulled down toward
  the isolated-cylinder resonance as neighbours decouple). The isolated cylinder (Tier 1 reference) is a *different*
  quantity (a scattering resonance of one cylinder); the limit check $\Lambda\to\infty$ is **not yet performed** (PLANNED).
* TE is a strongly reflecting broad band whose $Q$ has a minimum near $\Lambda\approx2.8$–3; the ≥ 90 % band peaks near
  $\Lambda\approx2.8$ (5.9 % fractional). TE $R_\text{peak}\ge0.999$ everywhere (lossless).
* TM $Q$ has a pronounced maximum near $\Lambda=2.4$–2.6 and falls on both sides: ≈ 10² at 2.2 and 2.8, ≈ 4 at $\Lambda=5$. In
  the high-Q region the reflection line is narrower than 0.1 % of $f_0$, so the operating bandwidth is tiny but the
  response is a clean $R\to1$ filter. This behaviour is consistent with a mode that nearly decouples from the zeroth
  order (an accidental bound state in the continuum), but **we have not demonstrated** the mechanism (that would require
  a mode-symmetry/field analysis or a $Q(\text{parameter})$ scaling fit).
* Q values below ≈ 10 depend on the estimator (fit vs. FWHM differ by up to 2×; see `docs/verification_validation.md`).

### Convergence

See `docs/verification_validation.md`. Summary: $f_0$ changes by $\le5\times10^{-4}$ (relative) and $Q$ by ≤ 0.2 % across
resolution 20–60 and PML/margin/monitor-gap variations for the TM ($\Lambda=3$) and TE ($\Lambda=4$) cases. The
high-Q TM modes at $\Lambda=2.4$, 2.6 were **not** subjected to a resolution study, and the ringdown that gives their $Q$ is
marginally converged; treat their $Q$ values as order-of-magnitude.

### Exploratory: Bloch-wavenumber dependence (TM, Λ=3; `band_structure.py`) — IN PROGRESS

Reflectance maps and Harminv modes at eleven values of $k_y$ spanning the first Brillouin zone ($0\ldots\pi/\Lambda$) (`figures/tier2_bands_TM_L3.png`). The lowest
mode at $k_y=0$ has $f=0.2547$, $Q=64.5$ by ringdown, matching the reflectance-fit value (0.2555, 59.7–65.0). Its frequency
*at fixed $k_y$* first rises, then the reflection peak disperses downward with $k_y$ toward the light line. No claim about
$Q(k_y)$ scaling is made: Harminv returns duplicated and spurious modes in this scan and only the lowest-mode
behaviour has been cross-checked. The TE scan was **not run**.

## Provenance note

The stored runs were produced before the repository's first commit, so `meta.json` records `git_commit: null` and
`git_dirty: true`; the configuration hash in each directory name and the recorded library versions (MEEP 1.34.0, Python 3.11,
NumPy 2.4, SciPy 1.17) identify the exact setup. Reruns in a committed tree will record the commit.
